PRIVACY POLICY

We collect nothing.

Effective date: July 18, 2026

Synodic ("the app") is a moon-phase viewer for iOS. This policy explains what data the app handles. The short version: none.

Data we collect

Synodic does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-ins, and no forms.

How the app works

All moon-phase information — phase, illumination, and cycle position — is computed entirely on your device from your device's clock. The app makes no network requests and requires no internet connection.

Analytics and tracking

Synodic contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking technologies, and no third-party SDKs.

Permissions

Synodic does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or any other protected data.

Children's privacy

Because Synodic collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Synodic ever handles data differently, this policy will be updated before that version ships, with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: hello@synodic.app
